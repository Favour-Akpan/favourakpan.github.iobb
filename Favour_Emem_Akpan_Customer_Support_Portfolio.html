Portfolio Samples

How I would approach common customer situations

These are mock demonstrations, included to show communication style and problem-solving approach — not claims of work for a specific company.

01 · DELAYED ORDER

Listen → Verify → Investigate → Communicate → Follow up

“I’m sorry for the delay. I understand how frustrating it can be to wait for an order. I’ll check the current status and confirm the next step with you as soon as I have the information.”

CALM · CLEAR · ACTION-ORIENTED
02 · CUSTOMER COMPLAINT

Understand → Check information → Offer a solution

“Thank you for bringing this to my attention. I understand your concern. Let me review the details so I can confirm what happened and explain the available next step.”

EMPATHY · ACCURACY · OWNERSHIP
03 · PRODUCT QUESTION

Ask → Understand needs → Recommend

“I’d be happy to help you choose. Could you tell me what you mainly need the product for? That will help me guide you toward the option that fits your needs.”

LISTEN · CLARIFY · GUIDE
04 · FOLLOW-UP

Check back → Confirm resolution → Offer further help

“I’m checking in to make sure the issue has been fully resolved on your end. If there is anything else you need, I’ll be happy to help.”
